◆FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your social media questions, answered.

What makes Groovemark different from the 50 other agencies offering social?

Most agencies run social like an assembly line: same templates, same captions, same monthly package across every client. We do the opposite. Your plan gets scoped to the platforms that drive your business, content sounds like you, and you keep the same point of contact month over month. You also own every post, graphic, and video we create. If you ever leave, it all leaves with you.

How long until I see real business results, not just likes?

Visible improvement in the feed and engagement happens inside the first month. Real business results, like new customers, bookings, and inbound DMs from people ready to buy, usually land in month two or three of consistent posting. Timelines depend on where your presence is starting from. The faster a baseline exists to build on, the faster results compound.

Why hire Groovemark instead of a cheaper service or doing it myself?

Cheaper services post templated content that does not move your business. Doing it yourself costs eight to twelve hours a week, every week, forever. Groovemark costs you twenty-five minutes a month and ships work that is custom-designed for your business by people who understand local marketing.

What if I don’t like the first batch of content?

You see and approve everything before it posts. If something does not feel right, you tell us, we revise it. Nothing goes live without your sign-off, ever. If after the first month it is still not landing, you get a full refund of that month and we part ways with no hard feelings.

Do I need to supply photos, videos, or content ideas?

Only what you want to. We work with whatever raw material you have, build graphics from scratch, add custom video on Growth Social and Scale Social, and bring our own ideas based on what works in your industry. The more behind-the-scenes footage you can send, the more personal the feed feels. But nothing ever stalls waiting on you.

Which platforms should my business actually be on?

Only the ones your customers use. For most local businesses that is some combination of Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X, with LinkedIn added for B2B and professional services. On the first call we look at your audience, your competitors, and your goals, then recommend the smallest set of platforms that will move the needle. Being great on two beats being mediocre on five, every time.

Do I have to approve every post?

Only if you want to. Every month’s content calendar goes over for your review before posting starts. Once we’re in rhythm, most clients shift to a quick weekly skim instead of approving each post. You stay in control of tone, brand voice, and anything sensitive. Your involvement stays light the moment the trust is built.

Will this work for my industry?

If you serve local customers, almost certainly yes. The strategy adapts to your category. We have approaches that work for service businesses, restaurants, clinics, retail, and more. If you’re in a regulated industry like medical or legal, we’ll tell you on the discovery call exactly how we adapt the playbook.

Before you call

Straight answers.

Will people know it’s a computer?

If they ask, it tells them the truth: it’s an automated assistant for your business, and it can take their details and get them on your schedule. Most callers don’t ask. They want somebody to pick up, write down the problem, and tell them when you’ll be there. It does that in a voice that sounds like a person, not a robot menu. And it says only what you approved on our call. Most guys have it say “Jonathan will call you back with a price.” If you want it giving your service-call fee, it will.

Do I lose my number?

No. Your number stays yours. I set up forwarding so calls you don’t answer roll to the agent instead of voicemail. Or forward everything and let it screen for you. You can switch that with one text to me.

What about a real emergency?

You tell me what counts: a burst pipe, no heat in January, sparks from a panel. When a caller describes one of those, the agent rings you through right then. If you don’t pick up, it texts you “EMERGENCY” with the details and tells the caller you’re on your way to a phone.

What’s the $350 a month for?

Keeping it answering. The phone line, the recordings, the texts to you, and me listening in to tune how it handles calls. Plus unlimited changes: new hours, a new service area, a new thing you don’t do anymore, a new guy who takes the emergencies. Text me and it’s done. Up to 500 minutes of calls a month is included, enough for a busy shop. No web site needed. It answers the number you already have.

What if I don’t like it?

Then you don’t pay. You hear it on our call before you spend a dime. Once it’s live, if it ever stops earning its keep, text me and it’s off in 30 days. I don’t chase anybody.

Why it matters

The call you miss goes to the next guy.

You’re on a roof, under a sink, or up a ladder with both hands full. Your phone buzzes in your pocket. You’ll call back at lunch. But the guy with the leak doesn’t wait for lunch. He hangs up at the second ring of your voicemail and dials the next name on the list. That was a $1,250 job, and you never knew it rang.

It happens again at 9 at night. And on Sunday. Most people who hit voicemail don’t leave one. They just move on.

A phone agent answers every one of those calls like a good office manager would. Then it texts you who called and what they need.

Before you call

Straight answers.

Will people know it’s a computer?

If they ask, it tells them the truth: it’s an automated assistant for your business, and it can take their details and get them on your schedule. Most callers don’t ask. They want somebody to pick up, write down the problem, and tell them when you’ll be there. It does that in a voice that sounds like a person, not a robot menu.

Do I lose my number?

No. Your number stays yours. I set up forwarding so calls you don’t answer roll to the agent instead of voicemail. Or forward everything and let it screen for you. You can switch that with one text to me.

Will it quote prices?

Only what you tell it to. Most guys have it say “Jonathan will call you back with a price” and leave it there. If you want it to give your service-call fee or your hourly, it will. Nothing it says is a surprise to you; you approve the answers on our call.

What about a real emergency?

You tell me what counts: a burst pipe, no heat in January, sparks from a panel. When a caller describes one of those, the agent rings you through right then. If you don’t pick up, it texts you “EMERGENCY” with the details and tells the caller you’re on your way to a phone.

What’s the $350 a month for?

Keeping it answering. The phone line, the recordings, the texts to you, and me listening in to tune how it handles calls. Plus unlimited changes: new hours, a new service area, a new thing you don’t do anymore, a new guy who takes the emergencies. Text me and it’s done. Up to 500 minutes of calls a month is included, enough for a busy shop. If you ever get close, I’ll tell you before anything changes.

Do I need the web site too?

No. It answers your existing number no matter what. But the two feed each other: the web site gets you found, the phone agent makes sure the call turns into a job. Owners who run both pay $350 a month for each. No bundle games, no contract on either.

What if I don’t like it?

Then you don’t pay. You hear it on our call before you spend a dime. Once it’s live, if it ever stops earning its keep, text me and it’s off in 30 days. I don’t chase anybody.

Founder Jonathan Kaufman Connect on LinkedIn

Most owners think their problem is getting more leads. It isn’t.

The leads are already there. The calls coming in this week. The people searching your name. The ones who clicked on your competitor instead. They’re slipping through cracks you can’t see:

  • A phone that rings four times.

I spent fifteen years in retail banking, most of them as a Branch Manager working directly with local business owners and high-net-worth clients. I sat across the desk from hundreds of them. I learned what keeps them up at night, what they need from a partner, and why the ones who do what they promise stay in their clients’ lives for decades.

I built Groovemark to be the partner I wish my clients had found years ago. The kind of partner who picks up when you call, builds what they said they’d build, and stays accountable long after launch day.

We don’t just drive traffic. We build the system that catches it.

◆ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions you’re already asking.

How do I get a price and timeline for my project?

A thirty-minute call. We ask about your market, your customers, and what’s broken. You walk away with a one-page proposal with scope, timing, and fixed pricing. If we’re not a fit, you still get the leaks list.

Do I need all three services?

Every business is at a different starting point. Any one service, done well, moves the needle. That said, the three work best together: AI Systems make sure no lead goes unanswered, your website turns those leads into bookings, and social keeps you visible until they’re ready to call. The whole becomes greater than the sum of the parts. Our recommendation is built around your situation, not a package we’re trying to sell.

Will my business show up in ChatGPT and other AI search tools?

Yes. When someone asks ChatGPT for the best local business in your category, the answer comes from the same kind of content that ranks on Google: clear, fast, well-structured pages with real information about who you serve and what you do. We build every site to be found by both. This isn’t a future problem. Customers are asking AI right now.

What if we are not a good fit?

We’ll tell you on the first call. We’d rather turn down work than take on a client we can’t do great work for. If we’re not the right team, we’ll say so. Where we can, we’ll point you toward someone who is.

Do I own what you build for me?

If you want to own it, yes. Website, content, accounts, data, all in your name. Some clients want that from day one. Others would rather we run the whole operation so they don’t have to think about it. Both work. No lock-in either way.

What happens after the work is live?

Depends on what you signed up for. On an ongoing care plan, we reply fast and ship small changes quickly. The person picking up the phone is the same person who built it. On a one-time build, you own everything and bring us back hourly or per-project whenever you need. Either way, you stay supported.

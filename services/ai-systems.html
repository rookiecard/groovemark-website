◆FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your AI questions, answered.

What makes Groovemark different from the 50 other shops pitching AI?

Three things. First, we build only what will pay for itself, starting with the highest-impact task in your week. No giant upfront stack, no hype-driven deployments. Second, the AI works for you and we run it: we build, manage, and tune the system. The work it does is yours. The work of running it is ours. Third, every system ships with clear human-override rules and a kill switch, so you stay in control the whole way.

Which task should I actually automate first?

Whichever one is costing you the most time or revenue right now. For most local businesses that’s one of three: lead follow-up (every delayed reply is a lost job), review generation (social proof drives new customers you never see), or a chatbot that handles the same handful of questions you answer every day. On the first call we find the one fix that pays for itself fastest.

How long until a system is actually running?

A focused one-system build is often live in one week. A fully connected stack runs two to three weeks. Voice agents and multi-location depend on scope of work but can range from a few days to a few weeks. Every engagement gets a specific timeline in writing before any work begins. We start with what delivers value fastest, not what takes the longest. You see the first win early.

Why hire Groovemark instead of using one of the off-the-shelf chatbot or voice builders?

Off-the-shelf builders ship templates. Generic scripts, pre-recorded prompts, the same canned responses every other business uses. They can’t connect to your calendar properly, they can’t escalate the right way, and nobody’s tuning them when they get something wrong. Yours is custom-built around your prices, your hours, your tools, and trained on the conversations your customers are having, both chat and voice. We run it, we tune it weekly, we own the tech debt so you don’t.

How does the voice agent handle real customer calls?

The voice agent uses real-time conversational AI, so it sounds natural. No menu trees, no “press 1 for sales.” It’s trained only on what your business does, so if a caller asks something outside that scope (a complex situation, an angry customer, a question we didn’t anticipate), it tells them you’ll be in touch and texts you the call summary right away. No voicemail black hole, no robotic loops. If a caller insists on a human, the call routes straight to you or whoever you designate. Every call gets logged with a transcript so you can spot anything we should retrain it on.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. All AI systems are built on enterprise-grade providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, or both depending on the tier) with zero-data-retention agreements where we can negotiate them. Customer conversations are stored in databases in your account, in your name, that you can export or delete anytime. We sign a data processing agreement with every client before any system goes live.

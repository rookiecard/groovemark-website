What I collect

When you call, text, email, use the chat on this site, or book a time with me, I get what you give me: your name, your phone number, your email address, your business name, and whatever you say in the message. If you become a client, I also keep the photos, job details and business information you send me so I can build and run your website.

This site does not use advertising cookies. It may use a basic analytics tool that counts visits and which buttons get tapped, so I can tell whether the page is doing its job. That tool does not identify you by name.

What I use it for

  • To call, text or email you back.
  • To build your preview and, if you say go, to build and run your website.
  • To send a review request to your customers on your behalf, if you are a client (details below).
  • To send you invoices and account messages.

I do not sell, rent or trade your information. I do not share it with anyone except the services I use to do the work (hosting, text messaging, booking and billing), and only as far as that work needs.

Text messages

If you text me or give me your number, I may text you back about your question, your preview or your website. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, and HELP for help. Your consent to receive texts is never a condition of buying anything.

Review requests sent for clients

If you are a Groovemark client, after you finish a job you enter your customer's first name and phone number into a form. One text goes to that customer, from your business, asking them to leave a Google review. The customer can reply STOP to receive nothing further. I use the customer's name and number only to send that request and keep a record that it went out. I do not use those numbers for anything else and I do not sell them.

How long I keep it

Messages and contact details stay as long as we are working together, or as long as I need them to answer you, plus what the law requires for business records. Ask and I will delete what I can.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts.
  • Email or text me to see, correct or delete the information I have about you.
  • Do not use the chat if you would rather not have the conversation stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date.

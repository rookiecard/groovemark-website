Before you call

Straight answers.

Do I own it?

Your name, your number and your Google listing are yours. The website is mine while I run it. That’s how the price stays at $350. If you ever leave, I’ll quote you a flat price to take it with you, before you say go, not after.

I’m not a computer guy.

Good. No logins, no dashboard. Text me photos of your work. When you finish a job, type the customer’s name and phone number into a simple form on your phone. That’s it. I do the rest.

I’ve got a Facebook page.

Keep it. Facebook is where your buddies see your posts. Google is where a stranger with a leak, a dead panel or a bad roof looks you up at nine at night, and if all he finds is a Facebook page, he calls the next guy. Already have a website? Text me the name anyway. The preview is free.

What’s the $350 a month for?

Keeping it working. Hosting, the chat answering at night, the review text after every job, your Google listing kept right. And unlimited changes: new photos, new hours, a new service, a new number, a new truck, a new guy on the crew. Text me and it’s done. No hourly bill, ever.

What happens on the call?

I text you the link, you open it on your phone, and we go through your site together. You tell me what to change: photos, services, towns, wording. Fifteen minutes. Then you decide.

What if I don’t like it?

Then you don’t pay. The preview is free, there’s nothing to cancel, and I don’t chase anybody. If you want changes, tell me and I’ll make them before you decide.

When does the phone ring?

Not tomorrow. A website doesn’t make people need you. It makes sure the ones who already do find you and not the next guy. Then the pieces start feeding each other. Every job sends a review request. Every review moves you up the next time somebody searches. The site turns that search into a call, and the chat catches the ones who look at night. One job becomes a review, a review becomes two more calls. Then it keeps building.

Will I be at the top of Google?

What I can do is give Google every reason to put you up there: a real site, a listing that’s kept right, and more reviews than the guy down the road. That’s the part you can control, and I run it for you.

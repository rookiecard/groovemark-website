The Second Shift system.

Your sales desk works first shift. Your web site stays open all night with nobody on the floor. Lead Capture works the second shift, and it does three things, in this order.

  1. 1. It answers.

    The buyer asks about stock, specs, delivery or financing. It answers from your web site and from what you tell me.

  2. 2. It gets the name.

    Once he has a real answer, it asks for his name, his number and what he’s after.

  3. 3. It hands him to your desk.

    Your sales desk gets a text with who he is and what he wants. It can book him a call or a visit too.

Straight answers.

Do I need a new web site?

No. It goes on the web site you already have. I add one line of code, or send it to whoever looks after your web site. Nothing else on it changes.

What does it tell buyers?

Only what you’ve approved. It answers from your web site and from what you tell me on our call: what you stock, what you service, where you deliver, how financing works. On price, you choose: quote it, give a range, or have your desk call back with a number. When it doesn’t know, it says so, takes the buyer’s name and number, and your desk answers.

Who gets the lead?

Whoever you say. One number, or split by department: sales, parts, service, rentals. Each lead arrives as a text with the name, number and what they want. I can send it by email too.

What if it gets one wrong?

Text me and I fix it, the same business day. I also read the chats and tune the answers every month, so it gets sharper the longer it runs.

Does it replace my salespeople?

No. It gets them names to call. It takes the questions that come in when nobody is at the desk and hands over a buyer who already knows you have what he needs. Your people do the selling.

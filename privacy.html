What I collect

What you give me. When you call, text, email, use the chat, call the demo line, fill out a form or book a time, I get your name, phone number, email address, business name, your trade, the towns you serve and whatever you say in the message. If you become a client, I also keep the photos, logo, job details and business information you send so I can build and run your service.

What the web site picks up on its own. Google Analytics records which pages you view, which buttons you tap, your device and browser type, your rough location (city level) and the link that brought you here. The companies that host the web site log IP addresses to keep it running and safe.

Payments. Card and bank details go straight to Stripe. I never see or store your full card or account number.

What I don’t collect. No Social Security numbers, no health information, nothing from kids, and nothing bought from data brokers.

Cookies and analytics

This web site has no ad cookies and no ad tracking. Google Analytics sets a first-party cookie (named _ga) so it can tell a new visit from a repeat one, and it loads a few seconds after the page does. Google signals and ad personalization are switched off.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics does not load at all. You can also block cookies in your browser or install Google’s opt-out add-on. The chat may keep a small ID in your browser so a conversation stays together if you come back.

The chat and the demo line

Mark, the chat on this web site, and Hank, who answers the voice agent demo line at 888-540-8003, are AI assistants, not people. They run on GoHighLevel, which sends what you type or say to AI model providers (OpenAI and Google) to write each reply.

Calls to the demo line are recorded and written out as text, and you get a text with a summary. I read chats and call notes to follow up with you and to make the assistants better. I do not use them to train AI models.

If you are a client with a voice agent, calls it answers for your business are recorded and written out so you get the details and so I can tune how it answers. Those recordings belong to your business and I use them for nothing else.

What I use it for

  • To call, text or email you back.
  • To book your call and send the reminders.
  • To build your preview and, if you say go, to build and run your web site, voice agent or social media.
  • To send review requests to your customers on your behalf, if you are a client.
  • To send invoices and account messages.
  • To see which parts of this web site work, so I can fix the ones that don’t.

I do not use your information for ads, for building profiles on you, or for anything you would be surprised to hear about.

Who sees it

I do not sell your information, and I do not share it for advertising. The only outside companies that touch it are the tools I use to do the work, and each gets only what that job needs:

  • GoHighLevel: contacts, chat, forms, booking, texts, calls, review requests and social posting.
  • Google: business email, Analytics and Google Business Profile.
  • OpenAI and Google: AI replies for the chat and the demo line, through GoHighLevel.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • PandaDoc: agreements and e-signatures.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare: hosting and running this web site.
  • Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter) and TikTok: only for social media clients, only to post to their own pages.

Beyond that, I would only hand information over if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or if Groovemark were ever sold, in which case the buyer has to keep the promises on this page.

Review requests sent for clients

If you are a Groovemark client, after a job you enter your customer’s first name and phone number or email into a form. A short request goes to that customer, from your business, asking for a Google review. The customer can reply STOP to receive nothing further. I use those details only to send that request and keep a record that it went out. For these customers I work on your behalf: the contact belongs to your business, and if one of them asks me to delete their details, I do it and let you know.

Social media pages run for clients

If you are a social media client, you give me access to your pages so I can post and answer messages for your business. I use that access only for that work. Your pages and everything on them stay yours, and you can remove my access at any time.

How long I keep it

  • If we never end up working together: deleted within 2 years of the last time we talked.
  • Chats and call recordings: kept up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Clients: kept while we work together. After you leave, I return or delete your files within 30 days, and keep only the billing records tax law requires (up to 7 years).

Ask any time and I’ll delete what the law lets me delete, sooner.

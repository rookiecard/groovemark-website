Why it matters

A dead page looks like a dead business.

Somebody’s neighbor says “call the guy who did our roof.” She looks you up on her phone. Your Facebook page comes up. Last post: three years ago, a photo of a truck. She wonders if you’re still around. She calls somebody whose page has a job from last Tuesday on it.

You’ve got better photos than that guy sitting on your phone right now. Finished panels, clean copper, a roof with the sun on it. They just never get posted, because you’re working.

Posting steady is a sign of a shop that’s busy. I keep the sign lit.

Here’s the deal

You text photos. I run the pages.

No agency with a content strategy deck. No app to learn. You text photos and a line or two about the job to one number. I write it up the way you’d say it, post it where people are looking, answer who comments, and send you a short note each month on what got the most attention.

What every package includes

  • Posts made from your job photos, written in your voice, with your phone number and town on every one.
  • Your Google reviews turned into posts. A happy customer’s words beat anything I could write.
  • Comments and messages answered in your voice. “Do you do water heaters?” gets a yes and your number, not silence.
  • A one-time setup: your pages built or cleaned up, with the right photo, cover, hours, number and a link to your web site.

Before you call

Straight answers.

I don’t take good photos.

You don’t have to. Stand back, hold the phone level, take three. I pick the best one, straighten it, brighten it, and crop it. A real photo of real work beats a stock picture every time, even a crooked one.

What if I forget to send photos?

I’ll text you a reminder. If a week goes by with nothing, I post from what you’ve already sent, your reviews, and things worth knowing about your trade: when to service a furnace, what a water heater sounds like before it goes. Your pages never go quiet.

Do my guys need to do anything?

No. One number gets the photos. If you want a foreman texting them instead of you, fine. Nobody logs into anything.

Will you post something dumb?

No. On our call you tell me how you talk and what you’d never say. For the first month, I text you every post before it goes up. After that, you can keep approving or let me run. Nothing political, no jokes at a customer’s expense, no stock photos pretending to be your crew.

Is TikTok really for a plumber?

More than you’d think. A 20-second clip of a drain clearing or a panel swap gets watched by people in your town who’ll need you next year. That’s why video sits in the Busy and Growth packages: it works, but it only works when it’s your real jobs on camera. I cut the clips. You just hit record for a few seconds on the job.

What’s the setup fee for?

Getting all four pages right before the first post: the same name, the same logo, the same phone number, hours, service area, and a link to your web site on each. A lot of the pages I see have an old number or no hours at all. That gets fixed first.

Do I need the web site too?

No. But a post makes someone curious and a web site is where they go to call. Owners who run both get a link to their site on every post. Each is its own month-to-month deal, no contract on either.

What if I don’t like it?

Then text me and it stops in 30 days. Your pages are yours and stay yours, with everything I posted still on them. I don’t chase anybody.

What it’s costing you

A dead page looks like a dead business.

Somebody’s neighbor says “call the guy who did our roof.” She looks you up on her phone. Your Facebook page comes up. Last post: three years ago, a photo of a truck. She wonders if you’re still around. She calls somebody whose page has a job from last Tuesday on it.

You’ve got better photos than that guy sitting on your phone right now. Finished panels, clean copper, a roof with the sun on it. They just never get posted, because you’re working.

Posting steady is a sign of a shop that’s busy. I keep the sign lit.

Before you call

Straight answers.

What will you post?

Things that make a customer pick you. Short tips your customers can actually use, like when to service a furnace, what a water heater sounds like before it goes, or why the breaker keeps tripping. Photos of jobs you’ve finished. Your Google reviews turned into posts. Plain facts about your trade, your service area and your hours. Every post comes with its own image, made by me, so you never have to hunt for a picture. Nothing political, no jokes at a customer’s expense, and for the first month you get a text to approve each one before it goes up.

Do I have to send photos?

No. Every post above runs without one. A fresh photo of this week’s job beats all of it, though, so send one when you’ve got it. Stand back, hold the phone level, take three. I’ll fix the rest.

Who answers the messages?

On Facebook and Instagram, the system does, around the clock, with the same answers you approved on our call: what you do, where you work, your number. Anything it can’t answer gets texted to you. Comments on X (Twitter) and TikTok I answer myself on weekdays in the Steady and Growth packages. Nobody on your crew logs into anything.

What’s the setup fee for?

Getting every page right before the first post goes up. The same name, the same logo, the same phone number, hours, service area, and a link to your web site on each. A lot of the pages I look at have an old number or no hours at all. That gets fixed first. No web site needed. If you have one, every post links to it.

What if I don’t like it?

Text me and it stops in 30 days. Your pages are yours and stay yours, with every post I wrote still on them. I don’t chase anybody.
